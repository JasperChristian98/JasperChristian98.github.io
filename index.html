GW3 · THE McDRAFT PREVIEW

We have entered the dangerous pre-GW3 optimism window. Squads have been tinkered with, receipts have been hidden and everybody is temporarily undefeated again.

There is a wonderfully unpleasant amount riding on danny’s doggy dudes v No Weimann No Cry. They sit 8th and 9th, level on 0 league points, so even a narrow win could have a very loud effect on the table.

Kamararama FC have a genuine springboard fixture. Get past Jaap? Best Stam and the current maths has them climbing from 4th to a provisional 1st. Mind the ceiling on the way up. There is proper leverage in this one for Buendophilia: take three points from NoRSNoRB No Chance and the live picture would bump them from 10th to a provisional 7th. Efficient little robbery, that.

Backstreet Moyes remain top dogs for now, but Jaap? Best Stam are level on points. A swing the wrong way and first place could develop a vacancy notice.

Jaap? Best Stam have swooped for Martinez from free agency, which is either inspired scouting or tomorrow's evidence exhibit. Backstreet Moyes have grabbed M.Bizot off the wire. New toy acquired; unreasonable expectations activated. Lewis-Potter is off the shelf and into Jaap? Best Stam. Nothing says 'new gameweek, new me' like immediate emotional dependence on a pickup. O.Dango has also been cut loose by Jaap? Best Stam, which is either ruthless squad management or the opening scene of a future Hall of Shame entry.

Free agency still has teeth: Ajayi, Tzolakis and M.Sangaré remain unattached and worth a look before somebody else decides they discovered them first.

The pieces are on the board for GW3. Now we wait for ninety minutes of football to make several days of careful squad planning look extremely silly.
